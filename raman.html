Raman spectroscopy

Vibrational spectroscopy for material identification, phase and structural analysis.

Discuss this capability
The technique

From principle to research.

Raman spectroscopy investigates the vibrational response of a material through inelastic light scattering. Spectral features can help identify materials and explore phase and structural differences.

Research applications

  • Material identification
  • Phase analysis
  • Structural characterization
