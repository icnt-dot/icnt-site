The technique

From principle to research.

The RAITH 180 system uses an electron beam to write patterns in a suitable resist. It connects nanoscale design with subsequent fabrication steps such as deposition and pattern transfer.

Research applications

  • Nanoscale research patterns
  • Device structure development
  • Lithography process studies
