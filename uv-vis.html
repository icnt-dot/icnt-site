The technique

From principle to research.

The УФ-6700 spectrophotometer records the optical response of a sample across ultraviolet and visible wavelengths. Suitable holders and reference samples are selected for each measurement.

Research applications

  • Optical transmission
  • Absorption spectra
  • Thin-film and solution studies
