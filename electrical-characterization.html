Ossila Electrical Characterization System

A probe station and measurement system for contacting research devices and studying electrical response.

Discuss this capability
The technique

From principle to research.

The Ossila system combines controlled probe positioning with electrical measurement equipment. It supports contacting pads and comparing device responses under an agreed measurement protocol.

Research applications

  • Device probing
  • Current–voltage measurements
  • Contact studies
